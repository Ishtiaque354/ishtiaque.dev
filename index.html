About

I work at the intersection of computer vision and medical image analysis — reconstructing 3D anatomy from 2D endoscopic video, and segmenting tissue from MRI volumes that rarely have clean boundaries. My undergraduate thesis turned colonoscopic footage into navigable 3D surfaces for clinical review; my current MSc thesis, RAVE-Knee, applies retrieval-augmented visual embeddings to multi-class knee cartilage segmentation.

I currently lecture in the Department of CSE at DIU, where I also mentor undergraduate students through the same thesis process I'm still navigating myself. Outside of imaging, I've contributed to a real-time air quality monitoring deployment and co-authored work in NLP and sentiment analysis.
