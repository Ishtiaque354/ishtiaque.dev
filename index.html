About

I study deep learning and computer vision, with a focus on medical image analysis and 3D reconstruction — building systems that turn 2D scans and video into inspectable 3D geometry. My work spans knee cartilage segmentation, colonoscopic polyp reconstruction, few-shot nuclei segmentation, and point cloud & mesh processing, broadly applicable to clinical imaging problems where accurate, interpretable 3D structure matters.

I am a Lecturer in the Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering at Daffodil International University, and an MSc candidate in Data Science at the same institution, advised by Dr. Md Zahid Hasan. My ongoing thesis, RAVE‑Knee, develops a retrieval-augmented visual embedding approach to knee cartilage segmentation — a continuation of my BSc thesis on geometry-aware 3D reconstruction of colonic polyps, now a first-author submission to Neural Computing and Applications.

Alongside my own research, I mentor early-stage researchers at the Health Informatics Research Lab (HIRL) and teach algorithms and theory of computation to undergraduates — work I see as inseparable from doing the research itself.
